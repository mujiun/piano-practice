Piano Practice

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Piano Practice is a piano sheet-music practice app. We designed it to work without collecting your personal information. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. We operate no servers that receive your data and we have no user accounts.

Data stored on your device

To make the app useful, the following is saved locally on your device only (in the app's local storage) and never leaves it:

  • Your practice progress and practice time
  • Pieces you save or recently opened
  • Preferences (language, dark mode, keep-screen-awake)

This information stays on your device. Removing the app deletes it.

Third-party content

  • Spotify previews. Some pieces embed an official Spotify player. If you play a preview, Spotify may process data under its own Privacy Policy. If you're offline, no preview loads and no data is exchanged.
  • Sheet music. Scores are shown from public-domain editions; viewing them collects no data.

Analytics

This version of the app includes no analytics or tracking. If we add anonymous usage analytics in a future version, we will update this policy and the app's App Store privacy details before doing so, and it will not be used to track you across other apps.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email youremail@example.com.

Summary: everything you do in Piano Practice stays on your device. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.
